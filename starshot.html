Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

The short version: to create your picture, the photo you choose is sent to our server and to an AI image service. It is used for that one generation and then deleted. We ask for no account, no name and no email, and we never use your photo to train anything.

This policy explains how the mobile application Starshot (ai.starshot.app) handles information. Published on Google Play by an individual developer.

The photo you upload

  • You choose a photo yourself — from the camera or the gallery. The app never reads your gallery on its own and never uploads anything you did not select.
  • The photo is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our server and from there to the AI image generation service that produces the picture.
  • It is used only to produce the image you asked for: not to train AI models, not published anywhere, not shown to other users, not sold or shared for advertising.
  • After the generation finishes the uploaded photo is deleted from our server. Results are stored temporarily so the app can show and save them, then removed automatically.
  • We do not run face recognition, build no biometric profile, and cannot link a photo to your identity — the app has no accounts.

Whose photo you may upload

This matters more than it might seem — for you as much as for us.

  • Your own photo. This is what the app is built for and the only case with no complications.
  • Someone else's photo — only with their clear consent. A person's face is their personal data in most countries. Uploading it without permission can breach privacy law regardless of what the app allows technically, and the responsibility sits with the person who uploaded it.
  • Photos of children — never. Not your own children either. There is no legitimate use of this app that requires a child's face.
  • Celebrities, politicians and other public figures — no. Placing a recognisable public person into a fabricated scene infringes their image and publicity rights, and in many jurisdictions is unlawful even when the image is obviously artificial. It also breaks Google Play's rules on deceptive content.
  • No one who could be harmed by the result. Do not create images placing a real person in a compromising, intimate, humiliating or misleading situation, and do not present a generated image as a real photograph of a real event.

You are responsible for having the right to use every photo you upload. If we receive a substantiated complaint about a specific image, we will remove it and may restrict access to the app for the device that created it.

Height and weight

Optional, so that generated body proportions look right. Stored on your device and sent with the generation request. Not tied to any identity, used for nothing else.

Device identifier

A random string created on first launch and stored on your device. It lets the server count your free generation and remember packs you purchased. It is not your Google account, not your phone number and not the advertising ID, and it disappears when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free generations require watching a short rewarded video. Advertising is supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect the Android advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate region is derived), device model and operating system version, interactions with the ads shown, and basic diagnostic data such as crash reports and load times.

Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; we receive only aggregate, non-personal reports. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and switch off ad personalisation in Settings → Google → Ads. Google's own terms apply: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Generation packs are sold through Google Play. Card details, billing address and Google account information are handled entirely by Google and never reach us. The app receives only a confirmation that a valid purchase exists.

What we never collect

No name, email or phone number. No account or password — there is no sign-up. No location, contacts, calendar, messages, files or browsing history. No access to your gallery beyond the single photo you pick for each generation.

Content and responsibility

Фотография, которую вы загружаете

  • Фото выбираете вы сами — с камеры или из галереи. Приложение не читает галерею самостоятельно и не отправляет ничего, чего вы не выбрали.

Идентификатор устройства

Случайная строка, создаваемая при первом запуске и хранящаяся на устройстве. По ней сервер учитывает бесплатную генерацию и помнит купленные пакеты. Это не аккаунт Google, не номер телефона и не рекламный идентификатор; при удалении приложения строка исчезает.

Реклама (Google AdMob)

Бесплатная генерация требует просмотра короткого ролика с вознаграждением. Рекламу предоставляет Google AdMob. Для показа и измерения рекламы Google может собирать рекламный идентификатор Android, IP-адрес (из него выводится приблизительный регион), модель устройства и версию операционной системы, взаимодействия с показанной рекламой и базовую диагностику — отчёты о сбоях и время загрузки.

Эту обработку Google ведёт как самостоятельный оператор; мы получаем только обезличенную сводную статистику. Сбросить или удалить рекламный идентификатор и отключить персонализацию можно в Настройки → Google → Реклама. Применяется политика Google: Как Google использует информацию сайтов и приложений.

Пакеты генераций продаются через Google Play. Данные карты, платёжный адрес и сведения аккаунта Google полностью обрабатывает Google — нам они не передаются. Приложение получает только подтверждение состоявшейся покупки.
